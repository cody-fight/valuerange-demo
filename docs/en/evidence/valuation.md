### 0.1 Company classification and valuation identity

Circle's most accurate current identity combines several roles in a clear order:

> **A rate-sensitive stablecoin issuer and regulated financial infrastructure business, currently earning primarily from reserves and defended by compliance and distribution. It is evolving toward a payments and financial-network platform, but evidence of platform value capture is not yet sufficient for a separately valued business segment.**

Reserve income accounted for about 95% of 2026Q2 total revenue. Coinbase distribution costs were about 48.6% of reserve income. Current earnings therefore still depend on average USDC supply, short-term rates and revenue sharing. Final OCC approval, CPN, Arc, Agent Stack, CCTP, Gateway and Mint demonstrate a real platform-development path. Without stable take rates, attributable revenue and segment gross profit, strategic presence cannot be equated with independent equity value.

### 0.2 Primary valuation structure

The sole primary structure is:

```text
2027E normalized reserve income = 2027E Average USDC × Reserve yield
2027E normalized total revenue = Reserve income + recurring non-reserve revenue (excluding Arc Token presale recognition)
RLDC = Normalized total revenue × scenario RLDC margin
Normalized Adjusted EBITDA = RLDC − Adjusted OpEx
Enterprise value = Normalized Adjusted EBITDA × consolidated EV/Adjusted EBITDA
Equity value = Enterprise value + eligible net cash
Per-share value = Equity value / 273.0m valuation diluted shares
```

A consolidated earnings multiple is used instead of quantitative SOTP because issuance, distribution, payments, cross-chain services and network products share the brand, compliance, liquidity, sales and R&D. Available data cannot reliably separate segment profits. Arc, CPN and Agent Stack receive no additional option-value sum. Their realization enters the nodes only through recurring revenue, RLDC margin, growth duration and valuation identity.

## 4. Primary valuation architecture and fit to available data

### 4.1 Why consolidated normalized EV/Adjusted EBITDA

1. RLDC is the observable net revenue pool after reserve income, Other Revenue and distribution costs; it reflects how Circle currently earns money.
2. Adjusted OpEx, R&D, sales, compliance and stock-based compensation are shared across products. Segment profits cannot be reliably separated.
3. An enterprise-value method explicitly bridges operating value to eligible net cash while excluding stablecoin-holder reserves.
4. A consistent normalized 2027E basis removes the revenue-quality distortion from one-time FY2026 Arc Token recognition.
5. Multiples vary with earnings quality, growth duration and platform identity. No separate network option is added, avoiding double counting.

Adjusted EBITDA excludes SBC. The model addresses this through valuation diluted shares, lower L0/L1 multiples and a discount for ongoing dilution risk. Unawarded award/ESPP pools are not treated as issued shares in advance.

### 4.2 Why no quantitative SOTP

Available data cannot reliably separate revenue, gross profit and shared expenses for the reserve core, CPN, Arc, Agent Stack, CCTP, Gateway and Mint. Applying revenue multiples to recurring products, an EBITDA multiple to the core and an additional network option would price the same platform growth through future revenue, multiples and options. A conceptual value map is retained, but the quantitative model uses only the consolidated primary anchor.

### 4.3 Why other primary anchors are not used

- **P/E:** large differences between GAAP and adjusted EPS, inconsistent share-count bases, and interest, SBC, token accounting and tax distort comparisons across reports.
- **DCF:** future rates, revenue sharing, platform take rates and long-term margins lack verifiable series; terminal value would dominate.
- **P/B or NAV:** holder reserves are not shareholder assets, and book equity is not Circle's primary earnings constraint.
- **P/S or a single RLDC multiple:** these fail to capture distribution costs, spending and earnings quality.

### 4.4 Secondary anchors and a change of valuation framework

- A consistent forward P/E checks whether consolidated EV/EBITDA implies an excessive or insufficient equity earnings multiple.
- Historical RLDC, Adjusted EBITDA and cash conversion check the scale of operating leverage.
- External price targets are diagnostic only, never inputs to L0–L3.

An auditable SOTP or multistage DCF becomes eligible if non-reserve businesses disclose revenue, take rates, gross profit, retention and separate expenses for four consecutive quarters and become material to consolidated profit. Until then, segment margins must not be fabricated.

RLDC margin fell from 59.93% in 2022A to about 39% in 2024A/2025A. Strong USDC growth does not automatically translate into proportionate shareholder profit; distribution mix and partner economics are structural variables. RLDC held stable in 2026Q2 while Adjusted OpEx increased, so platform investment has not yet demonstrated clear operating leverage.

### 5.2 Five value drivers

1. **Average USDC:** more directly determines reserve income than the ending balance.
2. **Reserve yield:** for each 100bps change, official sensitivity at the static 6/30 balance implies about $737 million of reserve-income change and $377 million of derived RLDC change.
3. **RLDC margin / revenue sharing:** consider reserve income, Other Revenue, Coinbase and other distribution costs together. Do not double count a rate change by modeling revenue and cost effects independently.
4. **Adjusted OpEx and dilution:** platform development creates value only if it becomes revenue or extends the duration of higher earnings.
5. **Platform monetization quality:** recurring charges, retention and gross profit from CPN/Arc/Agent determine whether valuation identity can upgrade.

## 10. L0–L3 operating states and full calculations

The common valuation period is normalized 2027E. Consolidated RLDC margin is `(Reserve income + recurring Other Revenue − distribution/transaction/other costs) / normalized revenue`. Scenario formulas approximate normalized Adjusted EBITDA as `RLDC − Adjusted OpEx`. The 2026Q2 difference was only about 1.0m (142.465m versus the official 143.478m), insufficient to justify extra fine-grained items. Amounts below are USD billions.

### 10.2 L0: normal bear floor

**Required conditions:** Average USDC only modestly exceeds the current level; yield falls to 2.5%; distribution competition compresses RLDC margin to 36%; platform spending continues without verifiable monetization, but consolidated EBITDA remains positive. USDC retains compliance, liquidity and 1:1 redemption. The core business thesis remains intact.

```text
Reserve income             = 78 × 2.5%                = 1.950000
Normalized revenue         = 1.950000 + 0.170000      = 2.120000
RLDC                       = 2.120000 × 36.0%         = 0.763200
Adjusted EBITDA            = 0.763200 − 0.640000      = 0.123200
Enterprise value           = 0.123200 × 12            = 1.478400
Equity value               = 1.478400 + 1.611883      = 3.090283
Per-share value            = 3.090283bn / 273.0m      = $11.32
```

**Main invalidation evidence:** Average USDC persistently below current scale, RLDC margin below about 32%, negative Adjusted EBITDA, or redemption/licensing problems. That moves the case into Stress Tail rather than continued use of L0.

### 10.3 L1: minimum base-scenario conditions

**Required conditions:** Average USDC reaches 95bn; yield is 2.9%; RLDC margin is 39%; recurring non-reserve revenue reaches 220m. Arc launches normally and CPN shows initial charging or retention without becoming an independent profit pool. Issuance infrastructure remains the main identity, supporting a 16x consolidated multiple.

```text
Reserve income             = 95 × 2.9%                = 2.755000
Normalized revenue         = 2.755000 + 0.220000      = 2.975000
RLDC                       = 2.975000 × 39.0%         = 1.160250
Adjusted EBITDA            = 1.160250 − 0.650000      = 0.510250
Enterprise value           = 0.510250 × 16            = 8.164000
Equity value               = 8.164000 + 1.611883      = 9.775883
Per-share value            = 9.775883bn / 273.0m      = $35.81
```

**Main invalidation evidence:** no evidence of platform charging, RLDC margin persistently below 38%, or expenses continually growing faster than RLDC means L1 is not met.

### 10.4 L2: minimum bull-scenario conditions

**Required conditions:** Average USDC reaches 125bn, near the company's through-cycle growth trajectory; yield is 3.2%; RLDC margin is 42.5%; recurring Other Revenue is 330m without dependence on one-time token recognition. At least one of CPN, Arc or other network products demonstrates recurring charges, retention and verifiable gross profit. Valuation identity starts shifting from issuer to financial-network platform.

```text
Reserve income             = 125 × 3.2%               = 4.000000
Normalized revenue         = 4.000000 + 0.330000      = 4.330000
RLDC                       = 4.330000 × 42.5%         = 1.840250
Adjusted EBITDA            = 1.840250 − 0.710000      = 1.130250
Enterprise value           = 1.130250 × 20            = 22.605000
Equity value               = 22.605000 + 1.611883     = 24.216883
Per-share value            = 24.216883bn / 273.0m     = $88.71
```

**Main invalidation evidence:** Other Revenue mainly from one-time token delivery, no improvement in distribution unit economics, or no recurring platform charges and retention prevents entry into L2.

### 10.5 L3: ceiling for full bull-case realization

**Required conditions:** Average USDC reaches 160bn; yield holds at 3.5%; RLDC margin reaches 45%; recurring Other Revenue is 480m. At least two businesses among CPN, Arc and Agent/CCTP/Gateway deliver scaled recurring revenue and gross profit, while owned distribution strengthens bargaining power. The platform transition is fully realized, but token FDV is still not attributed directly to CRCL.

```text
Reserve income             = 160 × 3.5%               = 5.600000
Normalized revenue         = 5.600000 + 0.480000      = 6.080000
RLDC                       = 6.080000 × 45.0%         = 2.736000
Adjusted EBITDA            = 2.736000 − 0.780000      = 1.956000
Enterprise value           = 1.956000 × 24            = 46.944000
Equity value               = 46.944000 + 1.611883     = 48.555883
Per-share value            = 48.555883bn / 273.0m     = $177.86
```

**Main invalidation evidence:** materially missing any condition for average USDC, yield, RLDC margin, recurring revenue or network monetization invalidates L3.
